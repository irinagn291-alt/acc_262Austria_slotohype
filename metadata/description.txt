Hypogeum is a once-a-day check-in for people who will do one action a day to go deeper, not a list that advances itself. Each day you align one pin on today's plate.

• Align today's pin — one pin per calendar day on the plate you hold
• Three pin seats on today's plate; the open seat reads Align
• Three pins in a row open the door and you drop one level
• A missed day clears the pins on the plate you hold
• Backslide — three missed days in a row lift you one level and lock the door above; points stay
• Weekly prize on every fifth level (Level 4, Level 9, Level 14, and so on); five pins in the device's calendar week open it. The pin that opens it is worth 20 points; a usual pin is worth 10
• NOW, POINTS, RANK, and RUN on the shaft rail
• Journal of dated pins, locked weekly prizes, and backslides, with Farthest
• Standing — held level, farthest reach, Pins, Streak, Longest streak
• Daily reminder at a chosen time. The notification title is Align today's pin and the body is Today's plate is waiting.
• Haptics on a seated pin
• Contact, Show the descent again, and Reset shaft

Marks, levels, points, reminder time, and haptics stay on this device.
