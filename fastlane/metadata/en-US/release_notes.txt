Version 1.0. Align today's pin on the plate you hold, one pin per calendar day. Three pins in a row open the door and you drop one level. A missed day clears the plate. Three missed days in a row lift you one level and lock the door above. A weekly prize waits on every fifth level.

The shaft shows NOW, POINTS, RANK, and RUN. Journal, Standing, Backslide, Daily reminder, Haptics, Contact, Show the descent again, and Reset shaft are in this release.
