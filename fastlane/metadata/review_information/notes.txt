Notifications appear when Settings applies the saved Daily reminder time, including when this screen is opened, and when the person changes Daily reminder. There is no custom usage-description string. The system prompt is the ask. If allowed, a repeating local reminder uses the title Align today's pin and the body Today's plate is waiting. The app does not ask for camera, photos, microphone, or location. A camera usage string exists in the build — This app does not use the camera. — and is never shown, because the camera is never requested.

There is no login, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Hypogeum is a once-a-day check-in for people who will do one action a day to go deeper, not a list that advances itself. Each day you align one pin on today's plate. Three pins in a row open the door and you drop one level.

No credentials or demo account are needed. A cold launch shows a system launch screen with no words, then a dark field. A spinner can appear. On a first install on a device, three onboarding panes follow. Skip stays in the top trailing corner. The counter reads Pane 1 of 3, Pane 2 of 3, or Pane 3 of 3. Pane 1 title The plate is the job. Body You sit on today's plate. A level only moves when a pin seats. Button Next. Pane 2 title Align today's pin. Body Three pins in a row open the door. A missed day clears the plate. Button Next. Pane 3 title Three misses lift you. Body You move up one level and the door above locks again. A weekly prize still waits on every fifth level. Button Descend. Skip and Descend both open the shaft and do not show the notifications prompt. On Simulator a first launch can skip onboarding and open an already-filled shaft; if that fill fails, use Retry. The shaft stays on screen. The left rail lists NOW, POINTS, RANK, and RUN. Tap the TODAY plate titled Align today's pin to seat today's pin. The open seat reads Align. A banner can read Today's pin is seated. or The door is open. You dropped one level. After one pin that day the plate reads Today's pin is already seated. Wait until the next calendar day. Open Journal from the closed-book control (VoiceOver: Journal). Before any mark it shows The path is closed. and Finish three days to open the next node. The button Align today's pin closes the sheet. After a pin, Journal shows Farthest and dated rows. Open Standing from the person control (VoiceOver: Hero). Before any pin: No pins yet. After a pin: HELD, Pins, Streak, and Longest streak. Open Settings from the gear control (VoiceOver: Settings) for Daily reminder, Haptics, Contact, Show the descent again, and Reset shaft. Opening Settings or changing Daily reminder can present the system notifications prompt. Reset shaft confirms with Reset the Hypogeum shaft? then Reset shaft or Cancel. Tap the BACKSLIDE card (Open the record.) to open Backslide. Sheets close with Close or a downward swipe. A later cold launch can flash a spinner or the first onboarding pane, then the shaft. Wait; that is not a new install unless you chose Reset shaft.

Marks, levels, points, reminder time, and haptics stay on this device. Contact in Settings is the support control; it opens the support page.

The app is English only. Today, missed days, and the weekly prize follow the device calendar, including the device's first weekday. Whole numbers on the rail and in Standing use the device's decimal grouping. Journal dates do not. Behaviour does not otherwise change by region.
